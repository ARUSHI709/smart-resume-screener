# Two-minute demo outline

1. Start the app with `python3 app.py`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
2. Upload a searchable PDF resume and show the confirmation with extracted skills.
3. Add a second resume by pasting text.
4. Paste a job description requesting Python, SQL, AWS, Docker, and four years of experience.
5. Click **Rank candidates** and explain the score, matched skills, gaps, and experience evidence.
6. Close with the responsible-use note: the shortlist supports a human reviewer and does not make hiring decisions.
